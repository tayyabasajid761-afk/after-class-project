Pizza can be a well-balanced meal containing protein (12g per slice on average), calcium from cheese, and various micronutrients.The tomato sauce is packed with lycopene, an antioxidant that helps reduce the risk of heart disease and supports skin health.

Pizza is energy-dense, meaning a few slices can easily exceed daily calorie needs, leading to weight gain.High levels of saturated fat from cheese and processed meats (like pepperoni) increase cholesterol, while high sodium (often >1,000 mg per serving) contributes to high blood pressure.
